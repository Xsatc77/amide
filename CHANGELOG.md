# Changelog

Newest first. Dates are when the work was done.

## v1.0.4 (2026-10)

- **Protocols: switch a line to another form of the medicine.** When a medicine is stocked in two or more mediums (B-12 drops and B-12 vials, for example), a **Switch to ...** button appears under it on the protocol card and on its Active Vials card. It points the line at the other form, with the route and dose you confirm, and keeps every dose already logged. It does not appear when there is only one form, and ended protocols cannot be switched.
- **Doses: undo now gives the unit back to the item it came from.** Undoing a drops or pills dose after the protocol line had been switched to another item used to return the unit to the new item and leave the old one short. Each logged dose now remembers the item it took from.
- **Orders: Add item to this order.** An item left off an order that was already submitted can now be added from the Orders tab, whether or not the order has been checked in. On a checked-in order the new item is counted as received straight away, and a supply adds to its count.
- **Orders: Edit and Delete order on the Orders tab.** Each item line has an **Edit** button that opens its edit popup, and **Delete order** removes the whole order after a confirmation (its lines, costs and lab reports; the items stay, and a checked-in supply order takes its stock back off the count). Editing was only on each item's own page before.
- **Links: new accounts start with a list of sites.** Every new account is given seven starter links (two community sites, a peptide list under Research, a podcast, a workout-routine site and two lab-testing sites). They are the person's own to edit or delete, and accounts that already exist are not changed.
- **Library: Add A General Note.** The My Notes page has an **Add A General Note** button that opens a popup for a title, an optional web link and a note that belongs to no peptide. General notes show as "General note" in the list, are found by search, and have a Delete button.
- **Journal: a refused photo upload now says why.** When Add A Photo was refused (a file Amide cannot read, too large, or a day already at 12 photos) the page reloaded with the reason up at the top and the popup half-open, so it looked like the photo had been accepted. The popup now reopens with the reason inside it, keeps the day and caption you chose, and the Upload button reads Uploading... and cannot be pressed twice. Photos are also now shrunk in the phone's browser and sent in the background, the way body photos already were, because a plain form post from a phone could arrive with no photo in it ("Choose a photo to upload") and nothing saved.
- **Labs: Import A Lab Report is a popup, and Charts start closed.** The import is now a button that opens a popup with a **lab** list (LabCorp, Quest Diagnostics, Any Lab Test Now!, myLAB Box, Direct Labs, Rythm Health, Function Health, Everlywell, RxHomeTest, DrSays, SiPhox Health, LetsGetChecked, Superpower, Jinfiniti, Another Lab), the PDF, and an optional **draw date** for reports that do not show one. LabCorp and Quest have their own readers; the others use a general best-effort reader, so check each imported line and use Edit to fix any. The **Charts** section on the Labs tab now starts closed and remembers if you open it.
- **Sign in: passkeys, a 30-minute session, and Remember This Device.** A **passkey** signs you in with Face ID, a fingerprint or your device PIN, with no password and no 2FA code, because it proves both the device and you; add one per phone or computer under Settings, **Manage passkeys** (it needs Amide's https address, and `AMIDE_PUBLIC_URL` fixes the address behind a proxy). Your face never leaves the device; Amide stores only a public key. The password and 2FA code still work as the fallback. The idle sign-out is now **30 minutes** (it was 10). Tick **Remember this device** on the 2FA code page and that browser skips the code for **14 days**; the password is still asked, and the device is forgotten when you turn 2FA off, change the password, have it reset, or press **Forget All Remembered Devices**. Migration 0081 adds the tables.
- **Labs: a reference for every marker.** Hover a marker name on the Labs tab for a one-line description of what it measures; click it to open a popup with the detail and what a value **above** or **below** the range can point to. It covers the markers on the New Panel list and the usual names on a lab report (cholesterol, thyroid, liver, kidney, blood count, vitamins, minerals, sex hormones, and more), and a ratio is described as itself, not as one of its parts. It is general information, not a diagnosis, and says so in the popup.
- **Check in: receive up to double the ordered quantity.** When a vendor sends extra vials (say, two more for shipping delays), **Check in** now accepts up to double the quantity ordered. Anything over the ordered amount **requires a note** saying why; at or under it the note stays optional. Migration 0080 widens the stored limit.
- **Dashboard: hover the Apple Health chart.** Hovering a point on the Apple Health card's chart now shows its date and distance, such as "Oct 12: 2.0 mi".
- **Labs: import a LabCorp report, star the labs your peptides recommend, and filter by peptide.** On the Labs tab, **Import A LabCorp Report (PDF)** reads the text version of a LabCorp patient report into a new panel: the collection date as the draw date, and every test's current result, units and reference range (the previous-result columns are skipped). Tests Amide already has a marker for (cholesterol, TSH, A1c, glucose and so on) use it; the rest keep LabCorp's name. The PDF is attached to the panel, and only the test rows are read, never the patient details. A date you already have a panel for is refused rather than doubled. A lab that a peptide in one of your **active protocols** lists under Recommended Monitoring gets a **star** (hover it for the peptide names), and a **Peptide** dropdown at the top of the tab narrows the results and the charts to just that peptide's labs.
- **Journal, Compare.** A new **Compare** button on the Journal tab opens a page where you pick a day of the week (and how far back) and see mood, energy, sleep quality and appetite for just those weekdays across your history: the dotted-line chart, a table setting that weekday against every day (averages, difference, lowest, highest, entries), and the average of each rating for all seven weekdays side by side to show a weekly pattern. Your own entries only.
- Journal: hovering a day's deficit or surplus bubble now shows the weekly estimate behind it, worded as on the Nutrition page ("about 4.0 lb per week if every day were like this: an estimate").
- **Dashboard: the Apple Health card is full width and charts your walking and running distance.** The last 14 days of the distance (when you left that metric on) are drawn beside today's steps and distance, in the same style as the Weight card.
- **Card titles are Title Case everywhere.** Every card, section and dialog heading in Amide starts each word with a capital ("Your Info", "Used In Protocols", "Water Against Your Goal, Last 14 Days"); units in parentheses such as (mg) or (lbs) stay lowercase. That includes titles built from labels in code (the Apple tab's chart titles, the Body charts, the Learn chapters) through a new Title Case helper; names you type yourself (a peptide, a plan, a vendor) are never changed, and the legal pages keep their standard form ("Terms of Service"). A test now fails if a template heading has a lowercase first letter.
- **TRT calculator for testosterone.** A new page (from the Calculator page and from any testosterone library card): vial strength (100, 200, 250 mg/mL or your own), weekly dose, and once weekly, twice weekly, every other day (3.5 a week) or daily give the mg and mL per injection, the U-100 units to pull on a syringe picture, the nearest whole mark and what it delivers, the volume used per week and, with a vial size, how long it lasts, plus a link that shares the result. Volume arithmetic only, never a dose recommendation.
- Dashboard, Items In Shipment: every other order now gets the deeper yellow vendor tab and a faint yellow wash across its row, so the orders read as separate blocks.
- **Shop this protocol also looks at liquids listed only by volume.** A peptide whose price list line is a volume with no strength ("B12 10ml*10vials") used to be left out of the plan. The shopping dialog now shows an **Also sold by volume** box asking for its strength in mg per mL; with it typed in, the plan prices that line by the mg in each vial (volume times strength), can choose it when it is cheapest, and plans no BAC water for it. The strength is part of the share text and the plain-text download too.
- **Premixed liquids.** A product listed with a strength per mL ("B12 1mg/ml", 10 mL vials) is premixed: its strength is no longer part of the name when matching (so it finds its library card), it is its own size in the card's best-price popup ("10ml premixed (1mg/mL, 10mg a vial)"), and the shopping plan can buy it using the mg in a vial (volume times strength) and then plans no BAC water for it. A liquid with only a volume and no stated strength stays a plain mL size and is never used in the plan.
- **B-12 is found under every spelling, and liquids sold by volume are no longer left out of the price list.** A vendor's B12, B 12, B_12, Vitamin B-12, Vit. B12, VB12, MB12, methyl B12 and the names of its forms (cyano-, methyl-, hydroxo-, adenosylcobalamin, mecobalamin) all match the B-12 card, while B-120, Vitamin B6 or "Lipo-C with B12" do not. The card's "Best price per vial" popup now also shows liquids listed as "10ml*10vials" as a size of their own (for example B-12 sold in 10 mL vials), never mixed with the mg sizes; before, those lines were dropped, so a vendor that lists B-12 by volume never appeared. At start-up Amide also tries again to link price lines that matched no card, so lines left unmatched under an older rule are picked up after an upgrade.
- **Protocol alerts: a library dose range now also comes from a card's Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced dosing.** Cards written from the dosing template have tiers instead of a separate range, so the alert said "the library has no dose range to compare your dose with" for peptides that do have dosing. With no range of its own, a card's plain tier amounts (at least two, in mg or mcg) now give one: the lowest, the Intermediate dose and the highest.
- **Dashboard: the "Health App Integrations, coming in a future update" card is now a live Apple Health card** (your own dashboard only): setup steps before you start, a waiting note once a token exists, then when data last arrived and today's steps and walking distance, with links to the Apple tab and Settings.
- **Protocol Builder: Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced boxes beside View card.** Ticking one fills the dose, unit and time of day (and the frequency when the library's wording is a plain daily, every other day, weekly or as needed) from that tier of the peptide's library card, with a note saying what was filled in and what is left to set. A level the card has no dosing for is greyed out.
- **Dashboard, Items In Shipment: the vendor sits in a yellow tab down the left edge of each order**, in black, reading bottom to top (an order with no vendor says "No vendor"). On a phone, where the table stacks into cards, the tab becomes a yellow band across the top of each card.
- **Journal Trends are one chart.** Mood, energy, sleep quality and appetite are drawn together in one full-width chart, each as a dotted line in its own colour on a fixed 1 to 5 scale, with a legend. Where two or more lines would land on the same day and rating they are nudged a little apart so none hides another. The Entries table's **Shared by** column, which ran off the edge, now appears only when someone's entries are shared with you.
- **Apple Health: the right day, however often the phone sends.** The phone app can now run as often as you like (two or three times a day is plenty, the card says): every reading, workout, symptom and state of mind counts on the local day of where your phone was when it was recorded, so overlapping, repeated or late exports never double a day, and a day sent again from another time zone replaces the earlier one instead of being stored twice (with Hours grouping, a trip between zones whose hours do not line up, such as +05:30 and a whole-hour zone, can still store an overlapping re-send twice; Days grouping avoids it). Activity from two sources (the phone and a watch) is no longer added together; food and water from two apps still add up. An export that keeps failing on the server's side is given up on after 5 failed tries in a row (the phone is told it arrived; a resend by hand starts a fresh 5 tries), exports a crash left unread are picked up by the daily pass, and the storage limit can also free copies that will not be read again (a resend of such a copy keeps it again and reads it). The popup has **Cancel** and a close X, the card counts all the stored-only kinds (0 included), and **Revoke** asks first. Chart captions on the Apple tab show whole numbers without ".0".
- **Apple Health (Health Auto Export).** Your iPhone's Apple Health data can now flow into Amide: **Settings, Integrations, Apple Health** makes a token and shows the address and header line for the phone app, with the exact settings in order and a live status. Amide receives the app's JSON at its own address, keeps every export as it arrived (90 days) and builds a clean per-metric history; a popup on the first data lets you choose what to show, and an **Apple** tab on Body charts it with today's and the week's figures, Apple workouts, symptoms and state of mind. ECG, heart rate notifications, cycle tracking and medications are stored only. Apple data stays apart from your own measurements. A read function returns the history as plain data. The receiver takes posts up to 64 MB (`AMIDE_HEALTH_MAX_MB`), keeps at most 2 GB of raw copies per person (`AMIDE_HEALTH_RAW_QUOTA_MB`; the oldest already-read copies go first), allows 10 live tokens per person, and asks the phone to resend when an import fails on the server's side. The guides have a new Apple Health section.
- Journal: the **Appetite** badge colors are now reversed, since lower appetite is the better result: 1 is green and 5 is red (mood, energy and sleep stay higher-is-better). The color is worked out when the page is drawn, so every past entry changes too.
- **Both guides brought up to date.** `docs/USER_GUIDE.md` and `docs/GUIDE.md` now cover everything added since the last update (Learn, scale readings and import, journal photos, photo reminders, macro rings, the 14-day water table and the 90% rule, removing an item from an order, price correction, Active Vials, weight change labels, the Not established convention on library cards), and all 39 screenshots in `docs/guide/img` were retaken from fresh invented demo data (nine are new).
- **Weight chart: change between weigh-ins.** On the Dashboard's weight card and the Measurements weight chart, each day's weigh-in is labeled with what was lost (green, −) or gained (amber, +) since the day before, in your units and in numbers big enough to read on a phone. Several weigh-ins on one day (a smart scale gives a few) count once, by the last one, so the labels never stack as "0.0". The first point has nothing before it, and labels are spaced so they never overlap (at most the latest 8 that fit).
- Food tab water table: the percentage rounds down (a day at 89.6% no longer reads "90%" beside "Short of 90%"), and a note appears when no time zone is set, since each day then follows the server's clock and evening water can land on the next day.
- Food tab: **Water against your goal, last 14 days.** A table of each day's intake, that day's goal, the share of it and whether it met the 90% rule (today is shown but not counted), with a summary of how many finished days were compliant.
- Dashboard: the Water Goal fill now turns green from **90%** of the day's goal (it was 100%), matching the 90% the H2O compliance bar counts as a good day; yellow still starts at half.
- **Library: FST-344 (follistatin-344) card.** A full card in the library template, researched from the primary papers (the 2015 and 2017 follistatin gene therapy trials, a mouse study, a 2020 case series linking injected follistatin-344 to a retinal condition, the ACE-031 trial, FDA letters) with "Not established" wherever a value could not be verified. It is the same compound as the older Follistatin-344 entry, so that entry is renamed and filled (protocols and prices keep pointing at it) and the names Follistatin, Follistatin 344, FS-344, FS344 and FST344 all match it. When the base library adds a card, price lines that matched nothing are now linked to it.
- **Edit a logged workout, past ones too.** Every workout shown on a Journal day now has an **Edit** button: plan workouts reopen with what you logged (saving replaces it and takes you back to the Journal), and quick or free workouts reopen in their own editor. And when you submit a workout with exercises not ticked Done, Amide lists them and asks whether you are sure before saving.
- **Buttons look like buttons.** Secondary ("ghost") buttons now keep their border, so they no longer read as plain text; links that act as buttons (Back to a list, Track orders, Open in calculator, View attached report, Add photos, Edit on a journal day, Price correction, Price list inbox, My notes, Goal stacks, Watch / Find a demo and others) are real buttons; button rows (Journal, Inventory, Workouts) are tidy even rows that wrap on a phone; and popups' title bars and Cancel / Save rows now span the whole popup. The Journal's Add a photo is a button that opens a popup (on a day's row too). The top menu is unchanged.
- **Body-photo reminders.** Two weeks after you upload a body photo, Amide reminds you to take another; a new upload replaces the old reminder with one two weeks from that upload (nothing is stored, it is worked out from your photos). Every protocol's **start date and end date** also asks for body photos. They show as a **BODY PHOTO** line on the Dashboard (with a link to add photos), as a camera on the Calendar, and, if you turned on push reminders, as a 9:00 AM ntfy message once a day while one is due. A start or end reminder stops after three days.
- **Journal photos.** Add a photo to any journal day: **Add a photo** at the top of the Journal tab (pick the day, several photos at once, optional caption) or **Add photo** on a day's row. For a picture that caught your attention, something motivating, a scale report. Thumbnails sit in the day's Notes cell and open full size; each has Delete. Photos are private to you (never shown to someone your journal is shared with), cleaned of location and camera data, up to 12 a day, and included in backups (never in a Share file).
- Measurements: **type a scale reading in by hand.** The Log A Measurement dialog has a new "Numbers from a body-composition scale (optional)" section with a box for every number the scale reports (masses in your units). The Recent entries table shows each entry's scale body fat and muscle, every entry can be deleted, and the latest-reading card has a **Delete this reading** button.
- **Smart-scale import (Renpho).** On the Measurements tab, **Import from a scale** loads the CSV your Renpho app exports: every weigh-in becomes a measurement on its date, with the scale's own numbers kept in separate fields (body fat %, fat mass, muscle, skeletal muscle, bone, protein, body water, fat-free mass, subcutaneous and visceral fat, BMR, metabolic age, BMI, waist-to-hip). Each number is a choice in the Overview "Metric" list (under "From your scale"), a card shows your latest reading with the change since the one before, and the scale's body-fat estimate is shown next to the tape-measure (Navy) one. Importing the same file again adds nothing. A kilogram export is converted to pounds.
- **Price correction** (administrator, in Settings): lists every product on the current price lists with each vendor's prices, and a dropdown of every peptide to say what it really is. Corrections are saved for the whole list at once, apply to every stored list, and are remembered for lists imported later. Choose "Add as a new peptide" for a product the library lacks: after saving you get a list of the missing ones to add (with an editable name), and anything still unmatched is offered too.
- **Dual Chamber Cartridge.** A price-list size such as "10mg Dual Chamber Cartridge" is recognised, kept apart from plain vials (its own size and price in the card's price popup, never mixed into the per-vial price range), and shown as "10mg Dual Chamber Cartridge" in a card's sizes. Inventory has a new form, **Dual Chamber Cartridge** (dry drug and diluent in separate chambers of one cartridge): amount per cartridge, and each logged dose takes its share of one cartridge.
- Orders: **remove an item from an order that has not shipped yet.** On the Orders page each item of a waiting order has "Remove from order" (for when the vendor cannot send it). The other items stay. The form is pre-filled with the shipping unchanged (now shared between fewer items) and the tax reduced by the removed item's share; change either if the vendor charged differently. Once an order has shipped, or if it has only one item, it cannot be changed this way.
- Dashboard: the **Water Goal card's fill changes color with progress**: blue below half the day's goal, yellow from half, green from 90% of the goal (the same 90% the H2O compliance bar counts as a good day).
- Learn, "How to read a COA": a new **Consistency in mass is fundamental** part (a vial that is 150% over, 70% over or 10% under its label from lot to lot cannot be dosed to the microgram, and a big overfill is a quality-control red flag, not a bonus), a matching red flag, and an "Overfill" glossary entry.
- Measurements: **changing the Range no longer resets the Overview metric.** The chosen metric now stays put whichever dropdown you change first (it travels in the address, so a reload or a shared link keeps it too).
- Measurements: the **Nutrition** ring is now a choice in the Overview "Metric" dropdown (it follows the Range dropdown next to it) instead of a separate card.
- Protocol editor: **"Split a dose" into several times a day now saves.** The editor refused to save the same peptide twice, which is exactly what a split into two or more injections a day makes, so it failed with "Please fix the highlighted fields." The same peptide can now appear again at a different time of day (it is still refused at the same time of day). And the red banner now lists each problem, saying which peptide or step it belongs to, instead of only telling you to fix the highlighted fields.
- **Learn: a new section, "Break Out Yer 6-Guns, Welcome to the Wild Wild West".** Third in the tutorial: scammers are everywhere, legit vendors do not contact you first, start with vetted vendors and order small batches when you explore, customs clearance on both sides of the ocean, factory holiday closures, embargoes and changing rules, gray versus controlled and illegal, and what to do when a deal is too good to be true or someone is rushing you. Later sections moved down one number.
- Fiber pills: setting "Fiber in one dose" on an item now also counts the doses you already took from it (ones that have no fiber amount yet), so the Food tab, the Journal and the rings catch up. A dose that already has its amount keeps it if you change the number later.
- **Fiber in the rings.** Each macro ring (Food tab, Journal days, and the Measurements range ring) is sized by the grams you logged (protein, carbs, fat and fiber) with the day's logged calories in the middle, exactly as the nutrition tab has them. Fiber from a pill adds to the fiber slice.
- **Macro rings.** The Food tab and each Journal day now show a donut of what you ate that day, split into protein, carbs and fat. The Measurements tab has a "Nutrition over ..." ring with its own range dropdown (the same ranges as the charts), showing the split over that period with grams in total and per day.
- **Fiber pills count toward fiber.** A medicine that is a pill, drops, etc. has a new "Fiber in one dose (g)" field (set it to the grams of fiber in a dose of, say, your psyllium husk pill). Each dose you log adds that fiber to the day's fiber on the Food tab, with a note saying how much came from the pill; undo takes it back, a skipped dose adds nothing. Amide does not guess the amount: enter it from the label.
- **Today screen: Quick Exercise and Journal buttons.** Quick Exercise opens the same quick-activity dialog as the Journal tab and brings you back to Today after logging. Journal opens today's journal entry.
- **Water compliance and the Journal now use each day's own goal.** A water log keeps the goal it was logged against, so raising, lowering or losing weight (which moves the default goal) no longer rewrites past days in the H2O bar. The Journal's day totals show that day's intake against its goal ("70 of 100 oz water"). Water logged before this update is judged against your current goal, as before.
- **Learn.** A new page in the account menu: a plain-language tutorial on gray-market peptides in twelve sections (what it is, risks and rewards, finding and vetting vendors with a tick-box scorecard saved on your device, reading a COA, vials, bacteriostatic water, syringes, what to do if you react, and the legal picture), with a glossary, a short "Terms in this section" box at the end of each section, and hover (tap on a phone) definition cards on glossary words. It includes a syringe-units teaching aid with made-up numbers. It is general education, not medical or legal advice, and it has not yet been reviewed by a clinician.
- Inventory: a medicine that is **not a powder vial** (pills, drops, salve, autoinjector, inhaler, liquid) has an **Active** checkbox in Add and Edit item. Active ones appear in the Active section next to the open vials with a picture for what they are (pill bottle, dropper bottle, salve tube, autoinjector, spray bottle), the same periodic-table style tile on the picture (the symbol leaves out the form word, so B-12 drops is B), a gauge of the units left of what has arrived, the lot being used on the green banner, and what the upcoming protocol will take, with a **Stop using** button. Migration 0069.
- Food, barcode scan on a phone: the camera now reads a barcode **by itself** (the phone's own barcode reader where it has one, otherwise the bundled scanner) and looks it up the moment it is found, with a status line while it works. A supply with orders shows its order history and Check in on its own page. Migration 0068.
- Inventory: **supplies are used up by shots and conversions.** A shot from a syringe takes 1 alcohol pad and 1 dosing syringe; a shot from a peptide pen takes 1 pad and 1 pen needle; converting an open vial to a pen takes 2 pads, 1 reconstitution syringe, 1 pen cartridge and 1 pen needle (loading a pen while reconstituting is unchanged). A logged shot is never refused for a missing supply, and undoing it gives them back. Migration 0067.
- Inventory: **doses now use up drops, pills, autoinjectors and other non-powder items.** Each logged dose takes its share of one unit (a unit is the item's amount, so a 5000 mcg dose from a bottle of 5000 mcg drops is one unit; a 2500 mcg dose is half). - Inventory, Active Vials: the lot banner is wider with larger writing, and the lot text is kept clear of the card's edges.

## Earlier releases

**v1.0 — Active Vials.** Reconstituting a Lyophilized inventory item (from the Inventory page or the Calculator) creates a tracked Active Vial — concentration, total content, discard-by date, and doses per vial, shown as a card with the real vial icon. Expired vials flag themselves for a one-time-per-24-hours discard prompt; discarding never deletes the record, just retires it. A default discard window (in days) is configurable in Settings.

**v0.9 — Sharing.** Per-person sharing, off by default: grant specific users a read-only view of your Inventory, your Protocols (personal data), or both, from the new Sharing section in Settings. Shared inventory merges into the viewer's own list, tagged by owner; shared protocols show in a separate "Shared with me" tab, never mixed into your own view or Calendar. Vendors are now a shared list across all users (like the peptide library) — what you bought from one stays private unless you share your inventory.

**v0.8 — Settings.** A Settings page (from the username menu) for changing your username/password, turning two-factor authentication on or off, setting an email (for future sharing features) and timezone, and picking a colorway — Light, Dark, or one of seven named palettes, including a pure-black-and-white High Contrast option. Admins get a Manage Users panel: add accounts, reset passwords, remove someone's two-factor authentication, or delete an account (which also removes everything they own, after two confirmations).

**v0.7 — Inventory Foundations.** The inventory form adapts to the medium: amount + unit (mg/mcg/IU), volume for Liquid, units-per-package for Autoinjector/Pill, plus expiration date and storage location, each required only where it makes sense. Vendors are now their own table (type a name to reuse or create it, like the peptide picker). The inventory list has search, medium filters, and sortable columns. Backup & restore (JSON + CSV export, additive-only import) is on the account menu.

**v0.6 — Reconstitution Calculator.** Live vial + BAC water + dose → concentration, draw volume and U-100 syringe units, with a syringe-fill visual, presets, and a reverse solver (pick the units you want, it works out the water). Pre-fills from Inventory (lyophilized items) or a Protocol's dose. *Not yet built:* turning a mixed vial into a tracked "Active Vial" that depletes inventory and has a discard-by date — that's still open, see the roadmap.

**v0.5 — Calendar.** Month, week and day views of every dose your active and scheduled protocols call for (titration steps included). Click any line, block or card to see exactly what's due.

**v0.4 — Accounts & legal notice.** Every visit after 10 minutes away starts with the legal notice, then New User / Login. Each user's inventory and protocols are private (the library is shared). Optional two-factor authentication with an authenticator app. See *Accounts* below.

**v0.3 — Peptide Library.** A searchable Library of your peptide cards (details as text plus the original card image), with your own dose range, frequency, aliases, notes and goal stacks per peptide. The protocol builder searches the whole library as you type. See *Importing your peptide cards* below.

**v0.2 — Protocols.** Build protocols from goals (suggested peptide stacks), set your own dose and schedule per peptide, optional titration steps, and see active protocols as cards; saved protocols can be paused, ended, repeated, or deleted.

**v0.1 — Inventory.** Add, edit, and delete inventory items (name, count, vial size, medium, lot/batch #, cost, vendor, order/shipped/arrival dates, COA photo/PDF with lab-measured vial size and purity, notes). See [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md) for what comes next.
