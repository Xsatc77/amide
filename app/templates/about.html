About Amide

I was a big one. A Mississippi redneck who blew up after military service to over 400 lbs. I tried every diet out there, and even spent nights looking for a legally (or illegally) sold tapeworm pill. Taking my kids to Six Flags and being denied the roller coasters was the straw that broke the camel's back.

In 2024 I had a vertical sleeve gastrectomy. My doctor had me set three goals: a beginning one, an intermediate one, and a shoot-for-the-stars one. 300, 250, and 211. 300 would be over 100 lbs down. 250 was my last weigh-in playing semi-pro football in California. 211 is what I weighed when I got married. I blew past 300, no sweat. I hit 254… and a brick wall. My weight loss plateaued for over two years. Not really going up or down, just stuck. I've hovered between 254 and 270 that whole time.

With everyone talking about GLP-1s and peptides, I started my research. I committed to buying some items and wanted to track it. Every tracker seems to want $9.99 a month, recurring. Nah, fam. You want us all to be healthy and fit, but only if we pay you. So I jumped on Claude. I'm not a coder or a programmer. This is the result of many months of research and a couple of weeks of prompts. I still don't understand how it all works… PFM, I guess.

Take it. Self-host it. Fill in as much or as little as you want. Keep your medical stuff as public or as private as you want. The choice is yours. I built the tool for me, but if you can get use from it, AWESOME!

I can't supply vendors for these things, legally, but they are available out there. If I can find them, you can too!

I don't want to charge you or make you sign up for a subscription or any of that nonsense. If you use this and like it, or see something you want added, let me know.
