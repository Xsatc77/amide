You've heard of peptides. You may have seen them in a forum, a group chat, or a friend's fridge. Welcome to the gray market, where almost every answer starts with "maybe."

Maybe the vial contains what the label says. Maybe it contains the amount on the label. Maybe it's clean. Maybe the person who sold it knew what they were doing, and maybe they only know how to build a website.

"Gray market" means these products are sold outside the regulated system. They are labeled "for research use only" or "not for human consumption." They haven't been approved for people, and nobody inspects them the way a pharmacy product is inspected. That label does not make human use legal or safe, and regulators have said so. The rules differ by country and by compound (see section 12).

This tutorial won't tell you what to buy or how much to take. It's about turning "maybe" into "probably," and about spotting the point where the honest answer is "walk away."

Your three jobs:

  1. Know what you're getting into.
  2. Check what you actually received.
  3. Keep yourself and your records in order.
