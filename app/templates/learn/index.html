Sources and review

This page was written as plain-language education from general knowledge of how peptide products, lab reports and injection supplies work. It is not a substitute for the sources below, and it has not been reviewed by a clinician.

Still needs expert review

  • Section 10, what to do if you react (a clinician).
  • Section 11, legal details by country. The US, UK and EU points rest on secondary sources, and how anti-doping rules classify some compounds is disputed.
  • Sections 5 and 6, on lab reports (someone with laboratory experience).
  • The benzyl alcohol and newborns wording, against a current package insert.

Where to check

  • Your country's medicines regulator (for example the FDA, TGA, Health Canada or MHRA) for what is allowed.
  • The current WADA Prohibited List, if you compete in a tested sport.
  • The package insert of any approved product, and a pharmacist, for storage and use.
